Step by Step

What Happens at Each Stage

Every step is designed to be clear, transparent, and easy to follow. There are no surprises.

1

Complete Your Membership Form

Start by choosing the Credit Pulse package that fits your goals. You will see the cashback amount, total package price, and biweekly payment breakdown clearly before submitting.

Fill in your basic membership details including your name, email address, phone number, and province. This step takes less than five minutes to complete.

No credit check is required to complete the membership application form. Credit Pulse is not a lender and does not issue credit products.

2

Secure Verification

After submitting your membership form, you will be guided through the required secure verification process. This step confirms your membership details and allows your cashback eligibility to be reviewed.

The verification process uses encrypted technology to protect your personal information. You will receive instructions on what is needed to complete this step.

Verification is required before cashback eligibility can be confirmed. The exact information required will be communicated clearly during this step.

3

Cashback Confirmation

Once your verification is reviewed, you will receive a cashback confirmation that shows the eligible cashback amount connected to your selected package, along with the payment schedule and next steps.

This step gives you a clear picture of your cashback eligibility before any payments are processed. You can review and confirm the details at your own pace.

The cashback confirmation is connected to the package you selected. Amounts vary by tier and are shown clearly before you proceed.

4

Receive Eligible Cashback

After completing all required steps � membership form, verification, and cashback confirmation � eligible cashback may be processed based on your selected tier and payment schedule.

Processing times may vary depending on your verification status, package selection, and payment provider. Credit Pulse will keep you informed throughout the process.

Cashback eligibility, timing, and results may vary. Credit Pulse will communicate processing status to members throughout the membership process.

1. Who We Are

Credit Pulse is an education-based cashback membership platform designed for Canadians. We provide access to financial education content, budgeting resources, credit education tools, membership materials, and eligible cashback benefits based on selected packages and completion of required steps.

Credit Pulse is not a lender, loan provider, credit card company, credit repair company, financial advisor, legal advisor, or financial product provider.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Credit Pulse, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification: Full name, email address, phone number, and mailing address provided during registration or checkout.
  • Payment Information: Credit or debit card details processed through our secure third-party payment processor. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Identity Verification Data: Documents or information provided to verify your identity as part of the cashback eligibility process.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent, clicks, and navigation paths.
  • Device & Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and referral URLs collected automatically via cookies and server logs.
  • Communications: Any messages, inquiries, or correspondence you submit through our contact form or support channels.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfil your membership registration and package selection.
  • Verify your identity and eligibility for cashback benefits.
  • Process payments and manage billing.
  • Deliver educational content, membership materials, and platform updates.
  • Communicate with you about your account, orders, and support requests.
  • Send promotional emails and offers (where you have consented).
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our website and services.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud or security issues.

4. Cashback & Verification

To process and fulfill cashback benefits, Credit Pulse may require identity verification. This may involve collecting a copy of a government-issued photo ID, proof of address, or other documentation as required to confirm eligibility and prevent fraudulent claims.

Verification documents are used solely for the purposes of confirming eligibility and are handled with strict confidentiality. They are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties except as required by law or to complete the verification process through authorized service providers.

Cashback amounts, eligibility conditions, timing, and results may vary. Completion of required educational steps is a condition of cashback eligibility.

5. Payment Information

All payment transactions are processed through secure, PCI-DSS-compliant third-party payment processors. Credit Pulse does not store, transmit, or have access to your full credit or debit card number.

We retain limited payment-related data (such as the last four digits of your card, transaction IDs, and billing amounts) for the purposes of record-keeping, customer support, and dispute resolution.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them to:

  • Remember your preferences and session state.
  • Analyze traffic and usage patterns through analytics tools.
  • Serve relevant content and improve site performance.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings, but doing so may affect the functionality of certain features on our website. By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

7. How We Share Your Information

Credit Pulse does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating our platform (payment processing, email delivery, identity verification, analytics) under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, court order, or government authority.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • Fraud Prevention: To detect, investigate, or prevent fraudulent or illegal activity.
  • With Your Consent: In any other case where you have explicitly authorized us to share your information.

8. Email & Communication

By providing your email address, you consent to receive transactional emails related to your account and membership (order confirmations, payment receipts, account updates). These communications are necessary to deliver our services and cannot be opted out of while you maintain an active account.

With your separate consent, we may also send marketing emails, newsletters, or promotional offers. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link in any marketing email or by contacting us directly. Opting out of marketing emails will not affect transactional communications.

9. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit, access controls, and secure server infrastructure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights and freedoms, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, maintain your active membership, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When your account is closed or your data is no longer required, we will securely delete or anonymize it in accordance with applicable privacy laws and our internal data retention schedules.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your province of residence, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent for the processing of your personal information at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the details listed in Section 16. We will respond to your request within the timeframes required by applicable law.
